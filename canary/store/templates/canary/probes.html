Payload canaries

A payload canary runs the production payload on one manifest row of a PCS task, as a canary task on the chosen queue: a test with a verdict, not a production run. Its two output files go to one flat dataset under epic:/TEST/ that expires after seven days; nothing lands under RECO, FULL or EVGEN. The verdict is a checklist read from the payload's own report: input, simulation, reconstruction, validation, registration, the payload exit, the events produced against those requested, and the output available in the catalog. It is collected on the next probe-dispatch cycle.
